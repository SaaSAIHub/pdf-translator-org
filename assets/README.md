# PDFTranslator.org Product Images

These assets accompany the [official product overview](../README.md).

- `pdftranslator-logo.png`: the product logo used on [PDFTranslator.org](https://pdftranslator.org/images/navbar-logo.png).
- `pdf-translator-online-homepage.jpg`: the English homepage captured on October 18, 2026, showing the upload form, language settings, style selector, and free allowance. The capture excludes browser chrome and task history.
- `research-paper-english.webp` and `research-paper-chinese.webp`: abstract and keyword excerpts from the research-paper comparison published on the product homepage. Author contact details were cropped out of both images; the text was not rewritten.
- `handbook-english.webp` and `handbook-chinese.webp`: the English handbook page and Chinese translation published in the homepage's handbook comparison.
- `textbook-english.webp` and `textbook-chinese.webp`: the English textbook page and Chinese translation published in the homepage's textbook comparison.

The comparisons are existing product presentation materials, not a new benchmark or a guarantee for every document. The two columns show English originals and Chinese translations; they do not establish a bilingual combined-download feature.

The images were checked for private account details and obsolete quota labels before publication. Keep filenames, language labels, and their README references aligned when updating an image. Do not replace actual translation results with generated artwork.
