# PDFTranslator.org Product Images

These assets accompany the [official product overview](../README.md).

- `pdftranslator-logo.png`: the product logo used on [PDFTranslator.org](https://pdftranslator.org/images/navbar-logo.png).
- `pdf-translator-online-homepage.jpg`: the English homepage captured in a guest session on November 25, 2026, showing the product logo, top navigation, language menu, sign-in button, and complete upload form with 30 free pages remaining and a two-task limit. The capture excludes browser chrome and task history; it waits for the live allowance to load.
- The Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese READMEs use guest screenshots captured from their respective live homepages on the same date: `pdf-translator-online-homepage-zh.jpg`, `pdf-translator-online-homepage-es.jpg`, and `pdf-translator-online-homepage-pt-BR.jpg`.
- `pdftranslator-membership-plans.jpg`: the live English pricing page captured on November 25, 2026, showing the free account and 30-/90-/365-day passes. Prices are a dated snapshot; refer to the website for current availability.
- `research-paper-english.webp` and `research-paper-chinese.webp`: abstract and keyword excerpts from the research-paper comparison published on the product homepage. Author contact details were cropped out of both images; the text was not rewritten.
- `handbook-english.webp` and `handbook-chinese.webp`: the English handbook page and Chinese translation published in the homepage's handbook comparison.
- `textbook-english.webp` and `textbook-chinese.webp`: the English textbook page and Chinese translation published in the homepage's textbook comparison.
- `vertical-japanese.webp` and `vertical-chinese.webp`: the [Japanese original](https://pdftranslator.org/before-after-case/before-after-case-3-0.webp) and [Chinese translation](https://pdftranslator.org/before-after-case/before-after-case-3-1.webp) from the homepage’s vertical-text example.

The paired images show individual pages from published product examples. The download menu for a completed task offers a translation-only PDF and a bilingual PDF.

The images were checked for private account details and obsolete quota labels before publication. Keep filenames, language labels, and their README references aligned when updating an image. Do not replace actual translation results with generated artwork.
