# PDF Translation Quality and Formatting Review

[Product overview and translation examples](../README.md) · [Translate a PDF](https://pdftranslator.org/) · [Usage guide](usage-guide.md)

PDFTranslator.org is designed to preserve a PDF's text layout, tables, images, and page structure during translation. Review the output alongside the original: translation can change text length, and complex layouts can require extra attention.

## Translate a PDF without losing formatting: what to check

Keeping the overall page structure is useful, but it is not the same as verifying every word or element. Inspect representative pages from the beginning, middle, and end, plus any page with an unusual layout.

| Area | Review questions |
| --- | --- |
| Text coverage | Are important passages present? Are any sections unexpectedly untranslated or repeated? |
| Reading order | Do multi-column paragraphs, notes, and captions follow the correct sequence? |
| Terminology | Are recurring technical terms consistent and appropriate to the subject? |
| Numbers and units | Do quantities, decimal marks, dates, currencies, and units match the source meaning? |
| Names and references | Are names, citations, identifiers, and cross-references recognizable and correct? |
| Tables | Do headings and values remain associated with the right rows and columns? |
| Figures | Do captions and relevant text correspond to the correct figure? |
| Formulas | Are mathematical expressions and their surrounding explanations readable? |
| Layout | Is text clipped, overlapping, unusually spaced, or pushed across page boundaries? |
| Fonts | Are all characters visible, including accents, punctuation, and non-Latin scripts? |

## Research paper translation

Start with the title, abstract, and key conclusions. Compare technical terms across sections, then check figure references, equations, units, and citations. A readable translated abstract is not evidence that every formula or table in the full paper is correct.

The [research-paper comparison in the overview](../README.md#research-paper-translation) shows an English abstract and its Chinese translation. Use that pair to examine paragraph structure and keywords; evaluate your own document separately.

## Translate PDF handbooks and manuals

Check numbered instructions, section headings, warnings, and ordered lists. A changed negation or unit can alter an instruction's meaning. Verify critical requirements against the original and the relevant authoritative instructions before using the result.

The [handbook comparison](../README.md#translate-pdf-handbooks-and-manuals) illustrates headings and lists in English and Chinese. It does not establish the outcome for every manual.

## Translate PDF textbooks and course materials

Compare the explanation with its diagram, exercise number, and caption. Check whether terms are consistent between prose and illustrations. For a translated chapter, sample different page types rather than checking only the first page.

The [textbook comparison](../README.md#translate-pdf-textbooks-and-course-materials) shows text, a concept box, and a diagram together. It is an English-to-Chinese page comparison, not a promise of a bilingual combined download.

## Scanned, vertical, and complex PDFs

Image-only scans may require OCR before they can be translated. Check the extracted text for omissions, mistaken characters, and incorrect order before translating. Low-quality scans and unusual fonts can make recognition harder.

For vertical text or multi-column pages, check the sequence of lines and blocks as well as the wording. Confirm how the current service handles your file instead of assuming that one successful page predicts the entire result.

## Report a specific issue

Use the [PDFTranslator.org FeedLog portal](https://pdftranslator-org.feedlog.ai/) for product feedback. Explain the document type, language pair, selected style, and the specific behavior you expected. If you can reproduce the issue with a non-sensitive document, use that instead of a private file.

Do not include personal documents, confidential passages, task identifiers, or signed download URLs. Documentation corrections can be submitted as a pull request; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
