# Current product facts and sources

Checked on **November 25, 2026**, against the public website after the v2.8.0.0 release. Recheck these facts whenever the service changes. This repository documents the hosted product; it is not the application source or a public API SDK.

## Access, pages and credits

| Access | Ordinary free pages per calendar month | Maximum per PDF | Queued + processing tasks |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Guest | 30 | 20 MiB / 200 pages | 2 |
| Registered account | 100 | 20 MiB / 200 pages | 2 |
| Active membership | 100 | 50 MiB / 800 pages | 5 |

- First registration: a one-time 1,000-credit gift, expiring on the matching date of the following month, or its final day if needed.
- Ordinary PDFs: use free pages first; additional pages cost 10 credits each for accounts. Unused free pages do not roll over.
- OCR: sign-in required; the whole document costs 20 credits per page, even if only some pages need recognition. Ordinary free pages do not apply.
- File inspection and the quote determine the actual cost. Required credits are confirmed before submission.

Sources: [homepage](https://pdftranslator.org/) and [pricing, including FAQ](https://pdftranslator.org/pricing). A fresh guest browser session showed 30 free pages and two task slots during the screenshot capture. Account and membership facts come from published pricing information; this documentation refresh did not perform purchases or account billing tests.

## Membership passes

| Pass | Validity from successful payment | Upfront credits | Displayed price (USD) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Month | 30 days | 10,000 | $9.90 |
| Quarter | 90 days | 30,000 | $24.90 |
| Year | 365 days | 125,000 | $78.90 |

Prices above are a dated snapshot of the live pricing page, not a permanent offer. Check the [website](https://pdftranslator.org/pricing) for current prices, supported payment methods and availability.

Passes are one-time purchases with no automatic renewal. Each purchase has its own validity; new purchases do not extend old credits, add remaining days or stack task limits. Membership adds priority and higher limits, without increasing monthly free pages. Unused pass credits expire with their own validity period.

Refund requests may be made within 24 hours of purchase and are manually reviewed; they are not unconditional. See the live pricing FAQ for eligibility and effects on remaining credits and membership.

## Translation and language entry points

- Published language coverage: **100+ languages**. Check the form for available source/target choices.
- Interface languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Vietnamese and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Downloads: translated-only PDF and side-by-side bilingual PDF.
- Styles: General, Academic, Legal and Business. Some languages do not support style selection.
- Layout preservation is supported; complex layouts, fonts and scans need review. No identical-layout, accuracy or fixed-speed guarantee.

## Preview and retention

Preview supports translated-only and bilingual modes. Download the result while the task remains available.

Task history remains visible for 24 hours after completion, failure or cancellation. Successful results can be previewed and downloaded for 24 hours after completion; membership does not extend the window. Guest history is browser-local; account history is managed by the service. Access expiry does not imply simultaneous physical deletion of stored files or business records.

Sources: public preview interface and [Privacy Policy](https://pdftranslator.org/privacy-policy).
